# Title: Bell’s Theorem in Quantum Magnets
Original Author: Ryan Buechele

*Subfield: Condensed Matter*

*Topics covered: Quantum information theory, Ising model*

*Type of project: Theory and Computation*

## Short Description:
This project will explore this idea of Bell's Theorem in the Quantum Ising model, a simple 1D toy model of magnetism. At the end of this project, the mentee will gain a working understanding of Bell’s theorem and the model, and along the way will learn some basic Python programming, elementary linear algebra, and basic quantum mechanics. Most of the challenging parts of the calculations done here will be pre-programmed, leaving the mentee to simply explore the results as different parameters are varied, and program a few simple functions and loops for collecting, saving, and plotting data. Before getting into the bulk of the work, the first few weeks will be dedicated to some instructional Jupyter notebooks which demonstrate the needed concepts and provide practice problems.
