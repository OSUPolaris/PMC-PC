# Title: Chemical Cartography of the Milky Way
Original Author: Liam Dubay
*Subfield: Astronomy*
*Topics covered: Stellar abundances, nucleosynthesis, Milky Way galaxy*
*Type of project: Data Analysis*

## Short Description:
The distribution of stellar abundances across the Galactic disk can tell us a lot about the formation history of the Milky Way galaxy. In the course of this project, the student will learn about stellar abundance surveys and how to handle and plot large astronomical datasets. They will also learn the basics of stellar life cycles and how supernovae affect the chemical abundances of stars.
