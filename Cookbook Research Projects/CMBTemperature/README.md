# Title: Determining the Temperature of the Cosmic Microwave Background
Original Author: Melanie Zaidel

*Subfield: Cosmology*

*Topics covered: Cosmic microwave background, blackbody radiation, general cosmology*

*Type of project: Theory and Data Analysis*

## Short Description:
In this project, the student will learn about blackbody radiation in the context of the cosmic microwave background in order to determine the temperature of the oldest radiation in the universe. The main deliverables of this project include a derivation of the Planck Distribution, a plot comparing the Plank Distribution to the Rayleigh-Jeans Law and Wien's Distribution, and minimizing a $\chi^2$ to determine the CMB's temperature.
