# Title: Ravenous Voids: A Study of Black Hole Growth
Original Author: Melanie Zaidel

*Subfield: Astrophysics*

*Topics covered: Black holes, Eddington limit, differential equations*

*Type of project: Theory*

## Short Description:
In this project, the student will learn about the basic physics of black holes, focusing on accretion and exponential growth. First, the student will read and watch videos about the basics of black holes and discuss details with me. Next, we’ll discuss why black hole growth is an exponential process, and solve the exponential growth differential equation together for an arbitrary growth rate. After that, we’ll focus on possible growth rates, and derive the formula for the Eddington-limited accretion rate with variable efficiency. Finally, the student will investigate combinations of black hole seed masses and sub/super-Eddington accretion rates to see how early black holes may grow into the supermassive objects at the centers of galaxies. Time permitting, the student may also consider black hole mergers in light detail, or numerically solve the exponential growth equation for a time-dependent growth rate.
