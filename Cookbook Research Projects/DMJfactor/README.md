# Title: Outnumbered by the Invisible: Calculating the amount of Galactic Dark Matter
Original Author: Melanie Zaidel

*Subfield: Astrophysics*

*Topics covered: Dark matter, numerical integration, celestial geometry*

*Type of project: Theory*

## Short Description:
In this project, the student will learn about the basics of dark matter and focus on the
calculation of how much dark matter is in the Milky Way Galaxy. First, the student will watch
videos about the basics of dark matter and discuss details with me. Next, we’ll discuss
dark matter density profiles, and create a plot of a cored-NFW profile. After that, we will
discuss numerical integration in Python to evaluate the integral of the density profile and
determine the mass of dark matter in the Galaxy. Finally, the student is guided through a
derivation in celestial geometry to parameterize line-of-sight integrals for calculating the
column density of dark matter in different directions. Time permitting, the student may plot
these column densities on a skymap.
