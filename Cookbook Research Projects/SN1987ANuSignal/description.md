# Title: Constraining the Electron Neutrino Mass using SN1987a
Original Author: Melanie Zaidel
*Subfield: Astroparticle Physics*
*Topics covered: Neutrinos, supernovae, special relativity*
*Type of project: Theory and data analysis*

## Short Description:
In this project, the student will learn about neutrinos and supernovae in order to calculate an upper limit on the mass of the electron neutrino using neutrino observations from SN1987A. To do so, the student will be introduced to special relativity and order of magnitude estimates in astronomy.
