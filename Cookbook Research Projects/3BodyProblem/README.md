# Title: The Three-Body Problem: Exploring the Chaos of Gravitational Motion
Original Author: Chloe Zheng

*Subfield: Computational Physics*

*Topics covered: Newtonian gravity, orbital dynamics, numerical simulations, chaos theory*

*Type of project: Theory / Computational*

## Short Description:
In this project, the student will explore the famous three-body problem: how three objects move under their mutual gravitational attraction. First, the student will learn about Newton's law of gravitation and the basics of orbital motion, starting with the simpler two-body problem. Next, we will discuss why the three-body problem is more complicated and introduce numerical methods for solving equations of motion. The student will then use Python to simulate and visualize the trajectories of three gravitationally interacting bodies. Finally, we will explore how small changes in initial conditions can lead to dramatically different outcomes, introducing the concepts of chaos and the unpredictability of gravitational systems.
