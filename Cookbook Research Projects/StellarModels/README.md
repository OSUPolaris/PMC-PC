# Title: Stellar Models and the Chandrasekhar Limit
Original Author: Poulomi Chakraborty

*Subfield: Astrophysics*

*Topics covered: Stellar Structure and Evolution, Chandrasekhar Limit*

*Type of project: Theory*

## Short Description:
The aim of the project is to introduce the student to the physical models that describe the structure and evolution of stars, with a focus on the Chandrasekhar limit and its implications for stellar evolution, especially for white dwarf stars.
